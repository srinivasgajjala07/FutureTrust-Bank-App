A Quick Guide to Open a Savings Account Online

A savings account is a very common service offered by several banks globally. With a savings account, you can achieve goals like managing your wealth for the future or managing day-to-day expenses.

What is a Savings Bank Account?

A savings account is a basic account in a bank where you can deposit your extra income while gaining interest on the same, depending on the saving account interest rate. The interest earned gets credited automatically to your account every year on a quarterly basis.

A savings account is very helpful to manage your finances effortlessly. To open a savings account is a simple task that merely takes a few minutes. Besides, most banks have the same procedure. If you are interested in opening a savings account, here we are with detailed steps for you.

Methods to Open a Savings Bank Account

You can open a savings bank account in two methods:

  • In-Person: You can directly apply in person at the bank you desire. This is a simple way as you will get seamless assistance from a bank employee, who is specifically dedicated to this job.
  • Online: Alternatively, and most conveniently, you can quickly apply online. This step is easy and saves a lot of time. For opening a Savings Account online, all you need is a mobile number for initiating the process. Just submit the application and attach documents online instead of going to the bank yourself.

How to Open a Savings Account Online at ABC Bank

Following are the pre-requisites for opening the account through online mode:

  • Aadhaar card
  • Mobile linked to Aadhaar for OTP
  • PAN card
  • Recent scanned image of photograph (Size: Up to 1 MB)
  • Scanned image of address proof (Size: Up to 1 MB)
  • Scanned image of Proof of Identity (Size: Up to 1 MB)
  • Signature scanned (Size: Up to 1 MB)
  • Mobile to be carried in person to branch for OTP verification while account opening.

Ready? Visit our corporate website ABC Bank → Digital Banking → Self Service Banking → Online Account Opening → Saving Account or click here to open your savings account online.

Steps to Open the Account

  1. Fill all the mandatory details and upload the documents. After saving the application, a reference number is generated, and an SMS is sent to the mobile number entered in the Applicant Details column.
  2. The system prompts the customer to submit the application form. After submission, please visit the branch with the unique reference number and the KYC documents mentioned in the online application (originals along with photocopies) and two passport-size recent photographs for verification and submission.
  3. Wait for the bank executives to complete the further official process of verifying the application.
  4. After verification, make an initial deposit in your new savings account. The initial amount may vary depending on the minimum balance required. For a zero-balance account, there’s no need to deposit any amount.

After making the deposit, bank executives will provide you with a debit card and relevant documents. You can also apply for an online debit card.

Conclusion

And that’s it! With these simple steps and a few documents, you can open a savings account seamlessly.
