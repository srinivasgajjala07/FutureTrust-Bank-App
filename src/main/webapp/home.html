A Quick Guide to Open a Savings Account Online

A savings account is a common banking service that allows you to deposit money safely while earning interest.

What is a Savings Bank Account?

A savings account helps individuals store money securely while earning interest on deposits. Interest is usually credited quarterly depending on bank policy.

Methods to Open a Savings Account

  • In-Person: Visit the nearest bank branch and submit required documents.
  • Online: Apply through the bank's website and upload documents digitally.

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card
  • Mobile linked with Aadhaar
  • Photograph
  • Address Proof
  • Identity Proof

Steps to Open an Account

  1. Fill the online application form.
  2. Upload documents and verify OTP.
  3. Visit the bank branch for verification.
  4. Deposit initial amount to activate the account.

After verification, you will receive your debit card and account details.
